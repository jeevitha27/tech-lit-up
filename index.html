Where you can help

We listened to NGOs. Their practical needs include:

  • Scribes and readers for training sessions
  • Dance practice support and accompanists
  • Tech mentors and interview coaches
  • Communication trainers and confidence coaches

Not sure where you fit? Tell us your skills and availability in the form below and we will match you with an NGO that needs them. Whether you give an hour or every week, you help a talented person take the next step.
